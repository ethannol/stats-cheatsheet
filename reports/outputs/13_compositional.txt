  > In the Jura data the metals are trace elements (sum ~0.03% of the soil), so closure to
    the whole is weak and raw correlations are not dominated by the artefact. The
    variation matrix still adds information: co/ni is the most proportional pair (co-

    is about geology (which elements travel together), not just about avoiding an
    artefact.

  > In a compositional biplot the *links* between ray tips matter, not the rays: a short
    link between two elements means their log-ratio has low variance (they are
    proportional). Rock types separate along the log-ratio axes, which is the geochemical
    fingerprint a mining geologist uses for lithology classification from assays.
